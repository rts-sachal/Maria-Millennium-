About our school

Education Beyond the Classroom

At Maria Millennium High School, Johar Campus, we aim to provide a supportive environment where students can develop knowledge, discipline, curiosity and respect for others.

Use this section to introduce your school, its educational philosophy and the opportunities it provides to students and families.

  • Our vision: To be a welcoming and inspiring school where every learner discovers their strengths, develops a love of learning, and is prepared to contribute positively to society. We aspire to help students grow into confident, thoughtful, and responsible individuals.
  • Our mission: To provide a supportive, inclusive learning environment with meaningful teaching, curiosity, creativity, and high expectations. We aim to nurture each student's academic progress, personal development, and confidence through cooperation among students, teachers, and families.
  • Our values: We encourage respect, honesty, responsibility, kindness, discipline, and fairness in everyday school life. We value curiosity, perseverance, teamwork, and care for our community, helping students make thoughtful choices and treat others with dignity.

Our approach

Learning for Every Stage

Learning for today’s world means building strong foundations, asking thoughtful questions, using technology responsibly, and developing the confidence to keep learning as the world changes.

🧸

Early Childhood

Young learners build language, early numeracy, curiosity and social skills through stories, play, exploration and creative activities. We encourage children to ask questions, express ideas, work kindly with others and develop the confidence that makes learning joyful.

✏️

Primary Years

Students strengthen reading, writing, mathematics and scientific thinking while learning to solve problems, communicate clearly and work as a team. Age-appropriate digital awareness, creativity and practical activities help them become curious, independent learners who can evaluate information and use technology responsibly.

🔭

Middle & Secondary

Students deepen subject knowledge and develop critical thinking, research, collaboration and problem-solving skills. They are encouraged to explore real-world questions, understand digital citizenship and online safety, communicate their ideas, and build adaptability and responsibility for further study and a rapidly changing world.
